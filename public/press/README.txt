Product
Stargazing Hub - Sky Live

Summary
Stargazing Hub is an observing and astrophotography companion for iOS and
Android. It combines multi-model observing forecasts, offline night-sky photo
recognition, a multilingual terrain-aware star chart, and light-pollution
planning tools.
