01 - Observing forecast
Stargazing Hub combines a stargazing index, Moon context, hourly conditions, and
multiple forecast models to help users choose an observing window.

02 - On-device sky recognition
A real wide-field night-sky photograph shown with recognized constellation and
notable-object context. The original photograph does not need to be uploaded.

03 - Terrain-aware star chart
The Stargazing Hub star chart combines constellations, horizon, terrain, and
observing context in the same view.

04 - Light-pollution planning
The light-pollution map helps users compare locations alongside night-sky
planning layers and observing time.
