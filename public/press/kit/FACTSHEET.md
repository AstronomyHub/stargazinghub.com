## Editorial focus for August 2026

The August release focuses on a connected field-to-review workflow. Users can
import a real wide-field night-sky photograph, identify the captured region on
the device, and continue into the same field in the app's multilingual star
chart.

## Core observing workflow

1. Compare hourly and 15-day observing conditions across multiple forecast
   models.
2. Consider Moon, light pollution, terrain, and location before travelling.
3. Identify a wide-field night-sky photograph on the device.
4. Open the recognized field in the terrain-aware star chart.

## Important claim boundaries

- The original night-sky photograph does not need to be uploaded for local
  recognition.
- Forecasts and maps are planning guidance, not visibility guarantees.
- Dates, locations, and values in supplied screenshots are capture examples.

## Origin

Aaron Hsu began building Stargazing Hub in 2018 after 7Timer became unavailable
while he was planning a planetary-imaging session. Observing trips with friends
continue to shape the product around practical field decisions.
